Documents

Business and personal checklists that adapt to the return

CPA Genie separates S corp, C corp, LLC, and individual returns while still supporting connected business owners who file both business and personal returns together.

Document categories

  • Business returns: prior client copy, profit and loss, balance sheet, invoices, payments, W-2s, 1099s, expenses.
  • Personal returns: W-2s, bank statements, mortgage interest, stocks, donations, itemized expenses, other tax forms.
  • Final delivery: downloadable business and personal client-copy returns organized by tax year.

AI document assistant with CPA approval

Security

Separate product, separate data boundary

CPA Genie should use a separate Azure app, database, storage account, audit trail, and AI search index from the employee portal. That keeps payroll/employee workflows and tax-client workflows cleanly isolated.
